# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps build a welcoming community:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its users.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Money and safety

This project handles payments. Contributors must not propose or merge changes
that let a merchant charge more than a subscriber's authorized cap, or charge
outside the agreed schedule, and must not present the UI as if they could.
Reports of such issues are treated as security matters and are handled first.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, and chat channels) and in public spaces when an individual is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at the contact address listed
on the stellar-sub GitHub organization profile, or by opening a private security
advisory on this repository for anything involving funds. All reports will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the
privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the code of conduct may face temporary
or permanent repercussions as determined by the other maintainers.
